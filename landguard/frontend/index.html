What LANDGUARD does and does not do

Wording is intentional.

Does

  • Detects potential inconsistencies between records.
  • Produces explainable findings with evidence references.
  • Flags missing evidence (e.g. missing mutation record).
  • Maintains a tamper-evident hash chain of every verification.

Does not

  • Declare anyone the legal owner.
  • Call a transaction legally fraudulent.
  • Connect to real Bangladesh government databases in this prototype.
  • Use AI / ML / LLMs to score risk.

Prototype data only. Parcel IDs, owner references, deed numbers, and coordinates are synthetic and clearly labelled. Nothing on this dashboard constitutes a legal record.
